Why Serendib

Good software starts with a team that’s easy to work with.

  • Engineers, not account layers

    You work directly with the engineers building your product. No relay of account managers, just clear answers from the people who write the code.

  • Fixed scope, clear milestones

    We agree on scope, timeline and cost before work begins. Every milestone ends with working software you can see and test, so progress is never a guess.

  • Support after launch

    Launch is a milestone, not the finish line. We stay on to monitor, maintain and improve what we’ve built together.

Process

Five steps. No surprises.

Every engagement follows the same clear path, with a checkpoint at each stage, so you always know what’s done, what’s next and what it costs.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We dig into your goals, users and constraints, then come back with a clear scope, timeline and fixed quote.

  2. 02

    Design

    Architecture, user flows and interface design, tested with clickable prototypes before any production code.

  3. 03

    Develop

    Short sprints that each end in a demo, on a staging environment you can try at any time.

  4. 04

    Deploy

    Automated tests, security checks and a planned go-live, with monitoring in place from day one.

  5. 05

    Support

    Maintenance, performance tuning and new features as your users and business grow.
